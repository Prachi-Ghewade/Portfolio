view repo →
problem
Predict which telecom customers are likely to cancel their service.
approach
Cleaned and explored ~7,000 customer records, encoded categorical features, and compared Logistic Regression against a Decision Tree in scikit-learn.
result
Logistic Regression came out ahead, at ~79% accuracy.
